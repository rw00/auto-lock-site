Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Core Commitment: 100% Offline & Zero Data Collection

Auto Lock & Focus ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") is built on an uncompromising privacy foundation: we collect, transmit, store, sell, and monetize zero personal data.

The App operates entirely offline and does not connect to external servers, cloud storage, third-party analytics platforms, or advertising networks. Everything you do within the App stays strictly on your physical Android device.

2. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

To enforce intentional focus sessions, prevent mindless distraction, and display mindful breathing overlays, the App requires specific Android system permissions. These permissions are executed solely on-device:

Accessibility Service (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

The Accessibility Service is used strictly to identify the foreground package name (the app currently active on your screen). When a user-designated distracting app is opened or a continuous screen threshold is crossed, the service displays a mindful break or lock overlay.

What we NEVER do: We do NOT monitor, record, capture, or store typed text, passwords, messages, screen contents, credentials, or personal interactions. The service performs package-name matching exclusively on your local device.

Usage Stats Access (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

The Usage Stats permission allows the App to calculate your daily distracting app usage and enforce your daily budget (such as the 2-hour daily ceiling). All calculations are computed in real time on your phone. No usage statistics or logs ever leave your device.

3. No User Accounts & No Registration

Auto Lock & Focus requires no user registration, username, email address, password, or third-party OAuth login (e.g., Google or Facebook sign-in). You can use all core features immediately upon installation without providing any identity information.

4. Payments & In-App Purchases

Lifetime access to Auto Lock & Focus is purchased as a one-time fee ($29 / 29€) processed directly by the Google Play Store Billing System.

We never receive, process, or store credit card details, bank information, or billing addresses. Payment processing and purchase receipts are governed entirely by Google's Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Zero Third-Party SDKs & Trackers

Auto Lock & Focus contains:

  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or Amplitude).
  • No crash reporting trackers (no Bugsnag, Sentry, or Crashlytics uploading user logs).
  • No advertisement SDKs (no AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network).
  • No social tracking pixels or identifiers.

6. Local Data Storage & Deletion

Your preferences, custom app whitelists, schedule settings, and focus session history are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore within the App's sandboxed private storage.

Deleting your data: You can completely erase all data, configurations, and session records at any time by clearing the App's storage via Android Settings > Apps > Auto Lock > Clear Storage or by uninstalling the application.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because no personal information is ever collected or transmitted, the App complies fully with COPPA and GDPR provisions regarding minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect updates to the App or relevant Android regulations. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date.
