Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 3, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the Auto Lock-in Android application ("the App"), provided by its independent developer ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). By installing or using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Zero Data Collection by the Developer

We do not collect any personal data. The Developer does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, share, or sell any personally identifiable information ("PII"), usage statistics, behavioral telemetry, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or diagnostic logs.

The App operates entirely offline on your device. We do not operate backend servers, cloud databases, or user account systems.

3. Payment Processing by Google Play

Transactions on the Google Play Store are processed exclusively by Google LLC via Google Play Billing.

When you acquire or purchase the App on Google Play, Google collects and processes your Google Account email address, payment instrument details, billing address, and transaction history to process orders, issue receipts, prevent fraud, and verify your entitlement across compatible devices.

The Developer never receives or accesses your payment card details or raw billing credentials. Google acts as an independent data controller for billing transactions under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Android Permissions & On-Device Processing

To enforce screen time limits, overnight schedules, and breathing pauses, the App requests specific Android system permissions. All data accessed through these permissions is processed strictly on your device and is never transmitted externally:

Accessibility Service API (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Used solely to detect the package name of the active foreground app so the App can display a lock screen or breathing pause overlay when a restricted app is opened or a continuous-use threshold is reached.

The App does not read, log, capture, or transmit on-screen text, keystrokes, passwords, messages, notifications, or credentials.

Package Usage Statistics (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

Used solely to calculate aggregate foreground app usage durations locally on your device to enforce daily time caps.

5. Local Storage, Retention & Deletion

App preferences, restricted app selections, schedules, and timer states are stored exclusively in the App's private, sandboxed storage on your device using Android Jetpack DataStore.

You can permanently delete all local App data at any time via Android Settings > Apps > Auto Lock-in > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the App.

6. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising SDKs

The App contains no third-party analytics frameworks, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or cross-app trackers.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and California), you may have statutory rights to access, rectify, port, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Because the Developer does not collect, hold, or sell any personal data off your device, we do not maintain any user records subject to access or deletion requests. For billing records held by Google, you may exercise your data rights directly through your Google Account.

8. Children's Privacy

We do not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent under COPPA, GDPR, or local law).
