Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and protection of information in connection with the Auto Lock & Focus mobile application for Android ("the Application" or "the App"), provided by the independent developer of Auto Lock & Focus ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). By installing or using the Application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Zero Data Collection Policy

We do not collect any data. The Developer does not collect, request, record, transmit, store on external servers, process, share, lease, or sell any personal data, personally identifiable information ("PII"), usage statistics, behavioral telemetry, device identifiers, IP addresses, location data, or diagnostic logs.

The Application operates entirely offline on your local Android device. We do not maintain or operate any backend servers, cloud databases, or user account systems capable of receiving data from your device.

3. Payment Processing and Collection of Email Addresses by Google

While the Developer does not collect any personal or financial data, purchases of the Application license are processed exclusively by Google LLC through the Google Play Billing platform.

Google records your email address for payments: When you purchase the Application on the Google Play Store, Google collects and records the email address associated with your Google Account, along with your payment method details, billing address, and transaction history, in order to process your payment, issue order receipts, prevent fraud, and verify your lifetime license entitlement across your Android devices.

The Developer never receives or accesses your credit card numbers, bank account information, or raw payment credentials. Google's collection, storage, and processing of your email address and payment records are governed exclusively by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

4. Android System Permissions and Local On-Device Execution

To enforce screen time limits, overnight schedules, and focus interventions, the Application requests specific Android system permissions. All data accessed through these permissions is processed strictly in volatile memory or sandboxed local storage on your device and is never transmitted externally:

Accessibility Service API (android.permission.BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

The Accessibility Service API is utilized solely to detect the package name of the currently active foreground application so the Application can display a lock screen or mindful breathing overlay when a restricted application is launched or a continuous usage limit is reached.

The Application does not read, inspect, log, capture, or transmit on-screen text, keystrokes, passwords, personal messages, notification contents, or user credentials.

Package Usage Statistics (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

The Usage Access permission enables the Application to query aggregate foreground usage durations on your device in order to enforce daily screen time caps. All usage calculations are performed locally on your device.

5. Local Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

All user preferences, designated application lists, schedule configurations, and timer states are stored strictly within the Application's private, sandboxed internal storage directory on your Android device via Android Jetpack DataStore.

You retain full control over this local data and may permanently erase all stored configurations at any time by clearing the Application's storage via Android Settings > Apps > Auto Lock & Focus > Storage & Cache > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the Application from your device.

6. No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising SDKs

The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics frameworks, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or cross-application tracking SDKs.

7. Disclaimer Regarding Application Blocking and Missed Notifications

Because the Application is designed to restrict access to designated applications and display full-screen intervention overlays locally on your device, its operation may suppress, delay, or prevent you from viewing or receiving notifications, messages, calls, or alerts from restricted applications. As further detailed in our Terms and Conditions, we are not liable for any missed notifications, delayed communications, or any consequences resulting from application blocking.
